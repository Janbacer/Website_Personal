About Me

Hi, and thanks for stopping by my little corner of the Internet! I'm Jan, a curious and hardworking student who absolutely loves tackling challenges. Life has taken me to live in three different countries, and each one has added something unique to my journey.

I study Mathematical Engineering in Data Science at Universitat Pompeu Fabra in Barcelona and Informatics at the Technical University of Munich. I'm deeply interested in using data and programming to solve real-world problems, especially in health and technology. Right now I'm writing my Bachelor's thesis on physics-informed neural networks for scramjet intake design.

Outside of class I co-founded the UPF Engineering Hub, I tutor high school students in math and science, and I volunteer with KIDS Barcelona at Sant Joan de Déu Hospital, bringing young people's perspectives into medical research.
